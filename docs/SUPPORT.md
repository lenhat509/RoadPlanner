# Cadence Support

**How are breaks planned?** By default you never drive more than 3 hours without a 15-minute
break, so breaks come every 2–3 hours. Change the interval when planning ("Custom break interval")
or in Settings.

**Why does my plan show a warning?** When a deadline or a sparse area makes your preferences
impossible, Cadence relaxes them step by step (for example, a longer driving day) and tells you
exactly what changed. It never removes required breaks.

**Prices look different at the pump or hotel.** Fuel and hotel prices come from Google, Expedia
and the U.S. EIA and can change; estimates are marked "est.". Always confirm before relying on
them.

**Voice guidance stops when I lock my phone.** Allow location access ("While Using the App" is
enough) and keep the trip active. Check that the ringer volume is up.

**I closed the app during a trip.** Open Cadence and tap **Resume** on the home screen. Progress
is saved automatically. Cadence shows the route from where you are to your next stop; tap
**Start** when you're ready. The red **✕** in drive mode leaves navigation without ending the trip.

**Can I use Google Maps for navigation instead?** Yes: in drive mode open the menu (•••) and
choose **Open in Google Maps** for the current leg.
