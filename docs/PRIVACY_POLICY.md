# Waystage Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 13, 2026*

Waystage ("the app") helps you plan and drive road trips. We designed it to collect as little
data as possible.

## What the app uses and why

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Your precise location | To plan from where you are, give turn-by-turn directions, detect arrival at stops and reroute | Google Maps Platform (routing and places). Not stored by us. |
| Places you search for, and your trip's start, stops and destination | To find routes, gas stations, rest areas and hotels | Google Maps Platform; approximate stop locations and dates are sent to our gateway to fetch hotel rates (Expedia) and regional fuel prices (U.S. EIA). |
| Your car's fuel economy and preferences | To calculate fuel stops and costs | Stored only on your device. |
| Your saved trips and progress | So you can resume a trip | Stored only on your device. |

We do **not** require an account, and we do **not** collect your name, email, contacts, photos,
or identifiers for advertising. We do not sell data, track you across apps or websites, or use
analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Background location

While a trip is active, the app can keep using your location when the screen is locked or you
switch apps, so voice guidance continues. iOS shows a location indicator while this happens.
Location is not used when no trip is active. You can change location permission at any time in
Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Waystage.

## Third parties

* **Google Maps Platform** — maps, directions and place information.
  Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
* **Expedia Group** (via our gateway) — hotel rates near your planned overnight stops.
  Expedia Privacy Statement: https://www.expedia.com/privacy
* **U.S. Energy Information Administration** (via our gateway) — public regional fuel prices.

Our gateway processes requests in memory to return results; it keeps short-lived caches of
hotel and fuel prices (keyed by approximate location and dates, not by person) and standard
server logs (IP address, request path) for up to 30 days for security and abuse prevention.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Deleting your data

Delete saved trips in the app, or delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.
