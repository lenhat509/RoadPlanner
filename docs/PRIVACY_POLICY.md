# Waystage Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 17, 2026*

Waystage ("the app") helps you plan and drive road trips. We designed it to collect as little
data as possible.

## What the app uses and why

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Your precise location | To plan from where you are, give turn-by-turn directions (on your phone and on CarPlay), detect arrival at stops and reroute | Google Maps Platform, including Google's Navigation SDK while it guides you (see below). Not stored by us. |
| Places you search for, and your trip's start, stops and destination | To find routes, gas stations, rest areas and hotels | Google Maps Platform; approximate stop locations and dates are sent to our gateway to fetch hotel rates (Expedia) and regional fuel prices (U.S. EIA). |
| Your car's fuel economy and preferences | To calculate fuel stops and costs | Stored only on your device. |
| Your saved trips and progress | So you can resume a trip | Stored only on your device. For places from Google, only their Google place IDs, and their positions for up to 30 days, are kept; names and addresses are fetched from Google again when you open a trip. |

We do **not** require an account, and we do **not** collect your name, email, contacts, photos,
or identifiers for advertising. We do not sell data, track you across apps or websites, or add
analytics or advertising SDKs of our own.

## Google's Navigation SDK

Turn-by-turn directions are provided by Google's Navigation SDK, which runs inside the app while
you drive with directions. Google's terms for it are shown before your first drive. While it
guides you, it sends your location and route to Google, and it collects diagnostic and usage
data: crash and performance data, how navigation is used, and a device identifier. Google uses
this to provide and improve navigation, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. This data
goes to Google, not to us, and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Background location

While a trip is active, the app can keep using your location when the screen is locked or you
switch apps (or while it's shown on CarPlay), so voice guidance continues. iOS shows a location indicator while this happens.
Location is not used when no trip is active. You can change location permission at any time in
Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Waystage.

## Third parties

* **Google Maps Platform** — maps, place information and turn-by-turn navigation (Navigation SDK).
  Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
* **Expedia Group** (via our gateway) — hotel rates near your planned overnight stops.
  Expedia Privacy Statement: https://www.expedia.com/privacy
* **U.S. Energy Information Administration** (via our gateway) — public regional fuel prices.

Our gateway processes requests in memory to return results; it keeps short-lived caches of
hotel and fuel prices (keyed by approximate location and dates, not by person) and standard
server logs (IP address, request path) for up to 30 days for security and abuse prevention.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Deleting your data

Delete saved trips in the app, or delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.
