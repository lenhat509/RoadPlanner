No account, sign-in or purchase is needed.

To try the full flow:
1. On first launch, read "Before you start" and tap "Agree and continue".
2. Tap "Plan a road trip". Choose a start (or "Use current location") and a destination a few hours away, for example Los Angeles to Las Vegas, then tap "Plan my trip".
3. When there are 2–3 reasonable routes, they are all drawn on the map with their driving times; tap one to choose it. Compare the Fastest / Cheapest / Balanced plans, each with breaks, fuel stops and hotels for overnight trips.
4. Tap "Start driving", then "Start" for turn-by-turn voice guidance.

Location: used for directions, to detect arrival at a stop, and to plan from where you are. Background location and audio keep voice guidance running while the screen is locked during an active trip only; both stop when the trip ends.

Waystage doesn't book anything: hotel buttons open the hotel's or a partner's website. Fuel and lodging prices are estimates and are labeled as such. Routes and places come from Google Maps Platform.
