No account is needed. To try the full flow: tap "Plan a trip", choose a start (or "Use current location") and a destination at least a few hours away (for example Los Angeles → Las Vegas), then "Plan trip". Compare the Fastest / Cheapest / Balanced options and tap "Start trip" for turn-by-turn guidance. Location is used only for navigation and planning while a trip is active (background location keeps voice guidance running when the screen is locked).
