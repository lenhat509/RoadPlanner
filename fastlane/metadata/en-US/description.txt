Waystage plans long drives the way a seasoned road-tripper would — then guides you there.

Tell Waystage where you're starting, where you're going, when you leave and how many miles your car gets per gallon. In seconds it builds a complete, safe itinerary:

• Breaks every 2–3 hours (or your own interval), so you never drive tired
• Fuel stops placed before your tank runs low, only at stations that sell your car's grade (Regular, Midgrade, Premium, E85 or Diesel), favouring cheaper ones
• Overnight stays near the end of each driving day, with live hotel rates where available
• An optional "arrive by" time that the plan respects whenever it's possible

CHOOSE WHAT MATTERS
Compare three plans side by side — Fastest, Cheapest and Balanced — with total time, distance, fuel, lodging and tolls. Pick one and see your trip day by day.

DRIVE WITH CONFIDENCE
Turn-by-turn voice guidance on Google Maps, a big, glanceable next-turn banner and a single panel showing your next stop and arrival time. When you reach a stop, one tap takes you to the next. Driving off route? Waystage reroutes automatically.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Your progress is saved automatically. Close the app, restart your phone, stay overnight — your trip resumes exactly where you stopped.

SAFE BY DESIGN
Safety rules come first: Waystage never plans more non-stop driving than your break interval. When a deadline can't be met safely, it tells you the earliest safe arrival instead.

Powered by Google Maps Platform for maps, routes and places, Expedia for hotel rates, and U.S. Energy Information Administration fuel price data. Prices and availability are estimates — always confirm before you rely on them.

Always obey traffic laws and keep your eyes on the road. Set up your trip before you start driving.
