Add stops on the way, like a park or a friend's town, with how long to stay, and Waystage plans the breaks, fuel and nights around them. Touch and hold any stop to remove it, keep it, pick another hotel or add a stop after it: the plan updates right away, and Undo brings it back. Running late? Adjust the rest keeps your plan and re-times it from where you are, or finds a hotel earlier tonight. Adjust visits, from the drive preview or the trip on Home, lists every stop ahead with its address and arrival time, and adds or removes places to visit.
